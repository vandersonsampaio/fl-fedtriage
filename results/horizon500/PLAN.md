# Plan — late collapse of `tri_all` up to 500 rounds (block D1)

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Status:** exploratory and descriptive, written before any run. It confirms nothing, and `tri_all`'s configuration does not change. Source: `plans/plano_acao_triage_2026-10-23.md`, §5, D1.

**Question:** when does the late collapse of `tri_all` on BloodMNIST start, and does it show up in cells other than the three where it was seen up to H = 300 (`sign_flipping` α 0.05; `label_flipping` and `low_mag_backdoor` α 0.1)?

## Grid

- BloodMNIST, logistic model, 10 clients (0 and 1 Byzantine), canonical root, server steps `native`, 500 rounds, `--log`.
- `tri_all` on the 19 attack cells, seeds 72–76 (95 jobs). This is the D1 grid itself.
- Two controls added to the action plan's grid, chosen before the runs:
  - `cos_bin` on the same cells (95 jobs), the best single signal at H = 250 in B1. It tells whether a collapse is specific to the AND rule. B1 found that `tri_all` lets in more Byzantine clients than `cos_bin`, which is the suspected mechanism.
  - `ceiling_fedavg10`, with no attack, 3 α × 5 seeds (15 jobs). It separates a collapse from drift that happens without attack.
- 205 jobs. `jobs.txt`, generated by `scripts.triage_grid jobs --datasets bloodmnist --systems tri_all cos_bin ceiling_fedavg10 --seeds 72 73 74 75 76 --rounds 500 --out results/horizon500 --log`, with the environment prefix removed because the dispatcher sets it.

## Definitions, fixed before the runs

- **MA10(r):** the mean accuracy over rounds r−9..r of one run.
- **Collapse of a run:** some round r ≥ 60 where MA10(r) is at least 5 p.p. below the running maximum of MA10 up to r.
- **Onset:** the first such round.
- **Late collapse:** a collapse with onset after round 250 (the confirmation's horizon).
- **Reported per cell and system:**
  - the number of seeds with a collapse by H = 300, 400 and 500, and the median onset;
  - the mean of (final MA10 − peak MA10);
  - accuracy at H = 250, 300, 400 and 500.
- **Reading rule:** a cell "has a late collapse" for `tri_all` if at least 2 of the 5 seeds show one. Such a cell is attributed to the AND rule only if `cos_bin` has a collapse in fewer seeds in the same cell, and the no-attack `ceiling_fedavg10` at the same α has no collapse in any seed. The inclusion logs (`--log`) are read for those cells: the rate of Byzantine clients included and the norm ratio, by window of 50 rounds, before and after the onset.

## Execution

- Runs on the two Azure VMs (D4as_v7, AMD EPYC 9V45), 4 processes per VM, 1 thread per process, `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=Haswell`, `ONEDNN_MAX_CPU_ISA=AVX2`. The VMs split the job list: odd lines on vm2, even lines on vm3. Estimate: about 29 min per job (B1 took about 17.5 min for 300 rounds on the same VMs), so about 12–13 h.
- **Determinism check:** rounds 1–300 of the `tri_all` and `cos_bin` runs must reproduce the B1 runs made on the VMs (`results/inclusion_mechanism/`, same cells, seeds and flags) with |Δ| = 0. B1 jobs made on the PC are not expected to match (`results/inclusion_mechanism/PLAN.md`). If the check fails, it is reported, and accuracy is compared only inside this grid.
- Accuracy is not pooled with `results/horizon300` (run on the PC).
