# Plan — `tri_all_sc`: capping only extreme norms among included clients (block D2)

**Date:** 2026-11-19, written before any run. **Authorized by the author on 2026-11-19, with k = 3 and the criterion below.** Exploratory, on development seeds 72–76; it confirms nothing, seeds 62–71 are not touched, and `tri_all`'s configuration does not change whatever the outcome. The result goes to the discussion and future work. Source: `plans/plano_acao_triage_2026-10-23.md`, §5, D2.

## Why

D1 (`results/horizon500/RESULT.md`) found one late collapse up to 500 rounds: `sign_flipping` at α = 0.05 on BloodMNIST (−25 p.p. from the peak, 5/5 seeds), with included Byzantine clients whose update norm reaches about 4× the honest median at rounds 200–250 and about 7× after round 250. Clipping every update to the median norm (`tri_all_clip`) removed the collapse but was rejected twice: −1.94 p.p. on average and −6.4 p.p. in one cell. D2 tests a much looser bound that only touches extreme norms.

## The variant

`tri_all_sc` is `tri_all` (S_R and server cosine, AND rule, b = 0.5, uniform weights, memory λ = 2) plus one step after the inclusion decision: every **included** client whose update L2 norm exceeds **k = 3** times the median norm of the included clients in that round is scaled down to that bound; nothing else changes. Implementation: `TriageLearner(norm_cap=3.0)` (`src/triage/learner.py::cap_included_norms`), system `tri_all_sc` in `scripts/triage_grid.py`. A round in which no included update exceeds the bound is bit-identical to `tri_all` (unit test `TestNormCap`; checked on a real BloodMNIST job before this plan).

**Why k = 3.** In the B1 logs (`results/inclusion_mechanism/raw/*/tri_all/*.log.csv.gz`; ratio = norm / median norm of the included clients in the round):

| Dataset | Honest included updates cut at k = 3 | Byzantine included updates cut at k = 3 |
|---|---|---|
| MNIST | 0.008% (honest q99.99 = 2.79) | 24% |
| BloodMNIST | 1.1%, concentrated in `sign_flipping` α 0.05 (11% there); honest q99.9 = 51 | 37% |

**Known risk, observed before the runs.** A smoke run (BloodMNIST, `sign_flipping` α 0.05, seed 72, 8 rounds, on the PC; not part of the grid) capped two honest clients (8 and 9) in every round from round 1 and none of the Byzantine clients, with accuracy from 13.5 p.p. below to 3.6 p.p. above `tri_all` in rounds 2–8 (below in 6 of the 7). In this cell honest norms are heterogeneous early in training, which matches the 11% above. This is the cell the variant is meant to fix, so criterion 1 and criterion 2's per-cell floor will decide whether this matters; k is not tuned after this observation.

## Grid

- `tri_all_sc` on the 19 attack cells, seeds 72–76, canonical root (100), server steps `native`, `--log`:
  - BloodMNIST, 500 rounds (95 jobs);
  - MNIST, 150 rounds (95 jobs).
- 190 jobs (`jobs.txt`), generated by `scripts.triage_grid jobs --datasets <ds> --systems tri_all_sc --seeds 72 73 74 75 76 --rounds <500|150> --out results/tri_all_sc --log`, with the environment prefix removed because the dispatcher sets it.
- **References, not rerun** (both made on the same two VMs, so comparisons stay within one machine type):
  - BloodMNIST: `tri_all` from D1, `results/horizon500/raw/bloodmnist/tri_all/` (500 rounds, same cells, seeds and flags);
  - MNIST: `tri_all` from B1, `results/inclusion_mechanism/raw/mnist/tri_all/` (150 rounds, same cells, seeds and flags).

## Definitions

The same as D1 (`results/horizon500/PLAN.md`): MA10(r) is the mean accuracy over rounds r−9..r of one run; **peak-to-end drop** is peak MA10 − final MA10 of a run; a run **collapses** when some round r ≥ 60 has MA10(r) at least 5 p.p. below the running maximum of MA10. Accuracy at H is the accuracy of round H. Δ = `tri_all_sc` − `tri_all`, in p.p., per seed and cell.

## Criterion (fixed before the runs)

`tri_all_sc` **passes** only if all three hold:

1. **The collapse goes away.** BloodMNIST, `sign_flipping` α 0.05, H = 500: peak-to-end drop ≤ 5 p.p. in at least 4 of 5 seeds.
2. **No meaningful cost.** Mean Δ over the 19 cells and 5 seeds ≥ −1 p.p. on BloodMNIST at H = 250 and on MNIST at H = 150, and no cell (mean over its 5 seeds) with Δ < −5 p.p. at those horizons.
3. **No new collapse.** On BloodMNIST up to H = 500, no cell other than `sign_flipping` α 0.05 where `tri_all_sc` has a peak-to-end drop > 5 p.p. in at least 2 of 5 seeds while `tri_all` (D1) has it in fewer than 2 of 5.

Otherwise it **fails**, and the failing condition is reported. Either way, `tri_all` stays the configuration of the thesis.

## Descriptive, not part of the criterion

- Δ at H = 250, 300, 400 and 500 on BloodMNIST, per cell and α, with CI95 over seeds (t, 5 seeds).
- The fraction of included honest and included Byzantine updates that were capped, and the mean scale factor, per cell and by 50-round window (from the `cap_scale` column of the logs).
- **Determinism check:** in each run, the rounds before the first capped round must equal the reference run with |Δ| = 0. Runs whose first cap happens in round 1 make this check vacuous and are counted.
