# Plan — `tri_all` and the cosine signals under FLTrust step parity

**Date:** 2026-11-18, written before any run. **Authorized by the author on 2026-11-18.** Descriptive, on development seeds 72–76; it confirms nothing, and seeds 62–71 are not touched. Source: the open item of `results/fltrust_parity/RESULT.md` ("`tri_all` under parity is still unmeasured").

## Why

`results/fltrust_parity/` moved only FLTrust to step parity (server steps = median over clients of ceil(n_i / 32) × local epochs). `tri_all`, `cos_bin` and `cosserver_only` also read the direction of the server update g₀, so both sides of H1 (`tri_all` vs the best single signal, BloodMNIST) and H4 (`tri_all` vs `cos_bin`, MNIST) of `results/f3_confirmation/` can move under parity. This grid measures them.

## Grid

- `tri_all`, `cos_bin`, `cosserver_only`, `--server_steps median`, root 100 (canonical), the 19 attack cells, seeds 72–76, `--log`.
- BloodMNIST 300 rounds, MNIST 150 rounds. 570 jobs (`jobs.txt`), generated by `scripts.triage_grid jobs --datasets <ds> --systems tri_all cos_bin cosserver_only --seeds 72 73 74 75 76 --rounds <300|150> --out results/tri_all_step_parity --log --server_steps median`, with the environment prefix removed (the dispatcher sets it).
- **Native references:** the B1 runs in `results/inclusion_mechanism/raw/` (same systems, cells, seeds, rounds, root and `--log`, all run on the same VMs; `sr_bin` is there too). B1 → D1 reproduced with |Δ| = 0 on the VMs (`results/horizon500/RESULT.md`), so the native side needs no rerun. PC runs are not pooled.
- `TriageLearner` saves and restores NumPy's RNG state around the server fit, so client streams are identical between native and parity: differences come from g₀ only.
- **Not included (cost):** root 20 (H1′–H3′). On the VMs it would need native runs too, about 28 more hours. It can follow if the reading at root 100 calls for it.

## Contrasts

Per seed, mean over the 19 cells, CI95 with t, 5 seeds; BloodMNIST at H = 250 (the F3 horizon) and H = 300, MNIST at H = 150. `@par` = parity, `@nat` = native (B1).

1. S@par − S@nat for S in `tri_all`, `cos_bin`, `cosserver_only`.
2. `tri_all@par` − best single signal under parity. The best single signal is chosen per dataset and horizon by the overall mean among `cos_bin@par`, `cosserver_only@par` and `sr_bin` (native; it does not read g₀, so it is valid under parity). Also reported against each of them.
3. The same contrast natively (`tri_all@nat` − best native signal), for reference.

## Reading rule

- **H1 side (BloodMNIST, H = 250) is at risk** if either holds: (a) the CI95 of contrast 2 is not entirely above zero; (b) its mean is below half of contrast 3.
- **H4 side (MNIST, H = 150) is at risk** if the lower bound of the CI90 of `tri_all@par` − `cos_bin@par` is below −1 p.p. (the F3 non-inferiority margin).
- **At risk** → the claim is reported as dependent on the server step mode, and a claim under parity would need a new confirmation on fresh seeds (author's decision). **Not at risk** → the claims are reported with both versions.

## Descriptive, not part of the rule

- Contrasts by α, and per cell.
- Inclusion rates under parity vs native (Byzantine included, honest excluded), as in B1.
- `tri_all@par` − `fltrust@par` using `results/fltrust_parity/raw` (run on the PC): cross-machine, so only as a rough reference.
- Late collapse with the D1 definition (MA10, 5 p.p.) on BloodMNIST up to 300 rounds.

## Execution

Two Azure VMs (D4as_v7), 4 processes each, 1 thread per process, `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=Haswell`, `ONEDNN_MAX_CPU_ISA=AVX2`, the same dispatcher and environment as B1 and D1, detached (`setsid`, `flock`, `@reboot`). Odd lines on vm2, even on vm3. Estimate from B1 timings (17.2 min per BloodMNIST job, 9.5 min per MNIST job): about 16 h.
